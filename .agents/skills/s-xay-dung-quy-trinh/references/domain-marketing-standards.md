## Marketing KPI Library

### Brand metrics
- **Brand awareness**: % target audience recognize brand (aided/unaided)
- **Brand recall**: % spontaneously recall brand
- **NPS** (Net Promoter Score): -100 to +100
- **Brand health index**: composite score (awareness + perception + intent)

### Campaign metrics
- **Reach**: unique people exposed
- **Frequency**: avg exposures per person
- **Impressions**: total exposures
- **Engagement rate**: interactions / impressions (% format)

### Digital performance
- **CTR** (Click-Through Rate): clicks / impressions
- **CPM** (Cost per Mille): cost per 1,000 impressions
- **CPC** (Cost per Click): cost / clicks
- **CPA** (Cost per Action): cost / desired action (lead, signup, purchase)
- **ROAS** (Return on Ad Spend): revenue / ad spend
- **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost): total marketing cost / new customers

### Content metrics
- **Views, reads, watches**: engagement volume per content
- **Time-on-content**: avg time consuming
- **Share rate**: shares / views
- **Conversion rate** (content → action): % consumers take next step

### PR metrics
- **Earned media value** (EMV): VND value of organic coverage
- **Share of voice**: % industry coverage about your brand
- **Sentiment score**: positive/neutral/negative breakdown
- **Media touchpoints**: # interactions with key journalists

## Marketing RACI Patterns

### Standard Marketing roles
- **CMO**: A on strategy, brand, major budget
- **Marketing Lead/Manager**: A on campaign execution, team management
- **Brand Lead**: A on brand health, creative direction
- **Content Lead**: A on content calendar + production
- **Performance Lead**: A on digital channels + measurement
- **PR Lead**: A on communications + crisis
- **Creative team**: R on creative production
- **Agency**: R on outsourced execution (per SOW)

### Cross-functional partners
- **Legal**: C on contract, compliance review (claims, IP)
- **Finance**: C on budget, A on spend approval
- **Sales**: C on go-to-market alignment, I on launch
- **BI**: C on measurement methodology, R on attribution analysis

### Common RACI patterns

**Campaign approval flow**:
- Marketing Manager (R) drafts campaign plan
- Brand Lead (C) reviews creative direction
- Legal (C) reviews claims/IP
- Finance (C) approves budget
- CMO (A) final approval for major campaigns; Marketing Lead (A) for routine

**Crisis communication**:
- PR Lead (R) drafts response
- CMO (A) approves
- Legal (C) reviews legal implications
- CEO (I) for major crises
- All staff (I) — internal communication

## VN Context — Marketing-Specific Patterns

### Tết Campaign Cycle (Massive in VN)
- **Pre-Tết** (Nov-Jan): biggest spend period of year. Campaigns launched 3-4 weeks before lunar new year.
- **Tết blackout**: 7-10 ngày Tết = minimum marketing activity (audience offline, agency closed)
- **Post-Tết** (Mar-Apr): brand awareness sustainability phase. Lower spend.
- **Mid-Autumn (Trung Thu)**: secondary peak for F&B, gifting categories
- **Cross-pollination with sales**: marketing Tết campaign PHẢI align với sales Q4 push timing

### Agency Management Patterns
- **Boutique vs International**: VN có 2 tier — boutique local (cheaper, faster, ít process) vs international (expensive, slower, formal). Choose per campaign type.
- **Relationship-heavy**: 1 agency lead "anh/em" relationship với client — agency switch costs (relationship rebuild) cao
- **Pitching culture**: agencies dùng "pitch free" để win — câu chuyện sau pitch khác xa. Build pilot vào contract.
- **Local content insight**: international agencies thường miss VN cultural nuances. Always local review for creative.

### Social Commerce VN
- **TikTok dominant**: cho Gen Z+Millennials. Livestream commerce >shop format
- **Facebook**: vẫn lớn cho gen 30-50. Shop hoạt động strong.
- **Zalo OA**: customer service + retention channel, ít cho acquisition
- **Shopee/Lazada/Tiki affiliate**: dùng macro+nano affiliates, không chỉ celebrity

### Performance Marketing VN
- **Attribution mess**: customer touch multi-channel (FB ad → Google search → TikTok review → Shopee buy) → last-click attribution mislead heavily
- **Cookieless transition**: GA4 + 1st party data heavier emphasis
- **Cost trend**: CPM Vietnam tăng 30%+ year-over-year. Budget plan phải có buffer

### PR/Crisis Management VN
- **Speed matters more than perfection**: VN social media moves nhanh, 24h là vĩnh viễn
- **Apology format**: VN audience expects nhận sai + giải pháp cụ thể + bồi thường (nếu áp dụng). Avoid corporate-speak.
- **KOL/Influencer crisis**: same playbook nhưng KOL involvement adds layer — coordinate response

## Common Marketing Process Pitfalls

1. **Brand vs Performance silo**: brand team focus awareness, performance team focus conversion — không nói chuyện nhau. Output: campaign disjointed. Solution: integrated weekly review.

2. **PCA theater**: Post-Campaign Analysis viết để có, không actually inform next campaign. Solution: PCA → input cho next campaign brief (mandatory).

3. **Agency over-reliance**: in-house team teaches nothing new, agency does everything → IP doesn't accumulate. Build in-house at minimum for: brand health, performance optimization, content strategy.

4. **Vanity metric trap**: report "ad got 1M views" without ROAS context. Solution: every output report PHẢI có cost + revenue/conversion link.

5. **Tết planning late**: campaigns approved trong Q4 → execution timeline rushed. Critical Tết campaigns PHẢI brief Q3.

6. **Creative bottleneck**: 1 brand manager duyệt creative cho 50 channels → bottleneck. Solution: tiered approval (low-stakes channels có pre-approved templates).

## Example: Worked SOP for MK-02 (Campaign Launch)

Khi user request enterprise-grade campaign launch SOP, follow 19-section template với:

- **Section V Glossary**: campaign types (brand vs perf vs hybrid), channel codes (FB/IG/TT/GG/YT), creative formats
- **Section VIII Approval**: tiered by budget (Mkt Mgr <500M, Mkt Lead 500M-2B, CMO >2B)
- **Section XI RACI**: 7 sub-processes × 8 roles (Marketing/Creative/Media/Finance/Legal/Agency/Brand/BI)
- **Section XIII SLA**: standard timeline 6-8 weeks for major campaign (brief → launch)
- **Section XVI Forms**: BM-MK02-01 Campaign brief, BM-MK02-02 Creative approval, BM-MK02-03 PCA template
- **Section XV Process relationships**: links to MK-04 (performance), MK-03 (content), Sales SL-07 (channel execution)
