## Sales KPI Library

### Top-line metrics
- **Doanh số (Revenue)**: VND, theo period (month/quarter/year)
- **Sell-in**: hàng vào kênh (warehouse, store inventory)
- **Sell-out**: hàng ra end customer (POS data)
- **GMV** (Gross Merchandise Value): cho TMĐT/Livestream
- **Achievement rate**: % vs target (90%+ = on track)

### Pipeline metrics (B2B/Project sales)
- **Pipeline value**: tổng giá trị deal đang open
- **Conversion rate**: % deals chuyển stage (per stage funnel)
- **Sales cycle**: average days từ first contact → close
- **Win rate**: deals won / deals attempted
- **Average deal size**: VND per deal

### Channel performance
- **ATV** (Average Transaction Value): doanh số / số giao dịch
- **Conversion rate Showroom**: % walk-in → buyer
- **Basket size**: items per transaction
- **Distribution coverage** (GT): số điểm bán có hàng / tổng điểm bán
- **Listing rate** (MT): % SKU được list trong chain
- **DOS/WOS** (Days/Weeks of Stock): tồn kho / sell-out rate

### Customer metrics
- **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost): cost / new customer
- **Retention rate**: % customers buy again in period
- **Churn rate**: 1 - retention
- **Customer lifetime value** (CLV): total value over customer lifespan

### Operational metrics
- **Order fulfillment rate**: % đơn giao đúng hẹn
- **Order accuracy**: % đơn giao đúng sản phẩm + số lượng
- **Return rate**: % đơn bị trả lại
- **DSO** (Days Sales Outstanding): công nợ days

## Sales RACI Patterns

### Standard Sales roles
- **GĐKD** (Giám đốc Kinh doanh): country-level, mostly A on strategic
- **GĐ KDMN/KDMB**: regional, A on regional execution
- **ASM** (Area Sales Manager): area-level, A on area execution
- **CHT** (Cửa hàng trưởng / Showroom Manager): SR-level, R on daily ops
- **KAM** (Key Account Manager): chain-level, R+A on KA accounts
- **Sales B2B / Dự án**: project sales, R on pipeline
- **Sales TMĐT**: e-commerce, R on online channels
- **Online Sales** (Livestream/Affiliate): R on social commerce

### Cross-functional partners
- **BI**: C on analytics, I on operations
- **Finance/Kế toán**: C on credit/AR, A on collection
- **Marketing**: C on campaign support, I on execution
- **CSKH**: C on customer issues, R on complaint handling
- **Cung ứng/Logistics**: C on availability, R on fulfillment

### Common RACI patterns by sub-process

**Pricing approval**:
- Sales rep proposes (R)
- Sales manager + Pricing Manager review (C)
- For >threshold deal: Finance review (C)
- Approval authority: depends on deal size (GĐKD for <500M, BGĐ for >500M)

**Quote → Contract**:
- Sales (R) drafts
- Pricing (C) + Legal (C) review
- Sales Manager (A) for standard deals
- GĐKD (A) for non-standard

**Order fulfillment**:
- Sales (R) takes order
- Warehouse (R) picks
- Logistics (R) delivers
- Sales Manager (A) for completion
- Customer (I) on shipment

## VN Context — Sales-Specific Patterns

### Tết Promotion Cycle (Critical for VN Sales)
- **Q4 buildup** (Oct-Dec): năm cũ push doanh số → channel stuffing risk (sell-in cao, sell-out không match)
- **Q1 slowdown** (Feb-Mar): post-Tết hangover. Sell-out yếu, inventory backlog
- **KPI scoring impact**: KHÔNG so sánh Q1 raw KPI với Q3. Apply seasonality adjustment.

### MT-GT Negotiation Patterns
- **MT** (Modern Trade): contract-based, slotting fees, return clauses formal
- **GT** (General Trade): relationship-based, credit terms flexible, return policies informal
- **Cross-pollination risk**: same product price differs across kênh → "kênh cannibalization" — customers buy where cheapest, supply chain confusion
- **Standard rule**: MT minimum price ≥ GT price + 10% buffer (else GT chuyển sang mua MT để arbitrage)

### Channel Conflict (Recurring VN issue)
- Showroom price vs Online price khác → customer phàn nàn
- GT distributor lấn vùng nhau → MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) violation
- Solution: explicit channel pricing policy + monitor with mystery shopper

### B2B Vietnamese specifics
- **Long sales cycle**: B2B average 4-6 months (vs 2-3 months in mature markets) — relationship building heavy
- **Decision committees**: rarely 1 decision maker. Often 3-5 stakeholders include "shadow influencer" (CEO's confidant, board advisor)
- **Tea/coffee culture**: face-to-face meetings essential trước contract. Don't try pure remote sales for >100M deals.
- **Credit terms**: 30-60 day payment standard. Aggressive credit collection backfires (loses customer).

### Showroom VN context
- **CHT autonomy varies**: small chains CHT có full P&L; large chains CHT execute strict SOPs. Build SOP accordingly.
- **Inventory bonus games**: CHT incentive on closing stock → quarter-end stuffing risk. KPI design phải có sell-out component.

## Common Sales Process Pitfalls

1. **Sell-in fixation**: KPI chỉ đo sell-in → channel stuffing, sell-out crash → return wave. Mandatory: sell-out KPI parallel.

2. **Distributor blame game**: doanh số yếu → blame GT distributor. Often actual root cause: pricing/product fit/competitor move. Investigate before action.

3. **Showroom theater**: KPI Showroom = walk-in count → CHT cheats với fake walk-ins. Better KPI: conversion + ATV.

4. **Pipeline sandbagging**: B2B sales hide opportunities until close to certain → quý-end surprise spike. Solution: pipeline review weekly, force stage transitions.

5. **Channel pricing chaos**: same SKU 3 prices ở 3 kênh → customer trust broken. Solution: unified pricing rule + exception process formal.

6. **Returns waste cycle**: return rate cao → root cause analysis weak → same defective product cycles. SL-11.08 (RCA) often skipped because no time.
