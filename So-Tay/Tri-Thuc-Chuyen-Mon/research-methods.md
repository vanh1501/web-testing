# Research Methods — PRO-W02 Domain Knowledge

## TL;DR
Pipeline: SCOPE → SOURCE → COLLECT → EVALUATE → SYNTHESIZE → DELIVER. Khác "search" ở methodology: triangulate ≥3 nguồn, grade credibility, show gaps rõ ràng. Answer trước, evidence sau (Pyramid).

## Research Pipeline

### Step 1: SCOPE — Phân rã câu hỏi
- Decompose câu hỏi lớn → 3-5 sub-questions (MECE)
- Xác định: decision nào cần thông tin này? Ai đọc? Depth cần?
- Set boundary: geography, timeframe, industry

### Step 2: SOURCE STRATEGY

| Research Type | Primary Sources | Secondary Sources |
|---------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Market Research | Industry reports (Statista, McKinsey), government data | News articles, blog posts |
| Competitor Analysis | Company websites, annual reports, job postings | Review sites, social media |
| Benchmark | Industry associations, consulting firms | Forum discussions, case studies |
| Policy/Regulation | Government gazette, official announcements | Law firm summaries, news |
| Technology Scouting | Vendor documentation, technical papers | Review sites, comparison platforms |
| Feasibility | Financial data, market data, expert interviews | Case studies, analogies |

### Step 3: EVALUATE — Source Credibility Grading

| Grade | Label | Criteria | Weight |
|-------|-------|----------|--------|
| A | Strong | Primary source, peer-reviewed, official, <1 year old | Full weight |
| B | Moderate | Reputable secondary, industry expert, <2 years old | 0.7× weight |
| C | Weak | Blog/forum, no citation, >2 years old, potential bias | 0.3× weight |
| D | Unreliable | Anonymous, contradicted by A-B sources, promotional | Exclude |

### Step 4: SYNTHESIZE
- Triangulate: mỗi finding cần ≥2 independent sources grade B+
- Conflict resolution: nếu 2 sources A mâu thuẫn → report cả hai + note conflict
- Gap detection: liệt kê thông tin CHƯA TÌM ĐƯỢC, explain tại sao quan trọng

### Step 5: DELIVER — Format theo type

| Output Type | Structure | Length |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Quick Brief | 5-7 key findings + sources | 300-500 từ |
| Research Report | Exec summary → Findings per sub-question → Gaps → Next steps | 800-1500 từ |
| Comparison Matrix | Criteria × Options table + recommendation | Table + 200 từ |
| Evidence Pack | Findings + full source list + confidence rating | 500-1000 từ |

## Quality Rules
- PHẢI triangulate: không kết luận từ 1 nguồn duy nhất
- PHẢI show confidence level (High/Medium/Low) cho mỗi finding
- PHẢI liệt kê gaps: thông tin chưa tìm được cũng là insight
- CẤM present opinions as facts — label rõ "opinion" vs "data"
- NÊN ưu tiên primary source hơn aggregator
