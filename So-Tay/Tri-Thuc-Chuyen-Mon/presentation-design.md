# Presentation Design — PRO-W03 Domain Knowledge

## TL;DR
1 slide = 1 message. Structure theo purpose (inform/persuade/train). Storytelling arc: Setup → Tension → Resolution. Visual > text.

## Slide Deck Structures

### Pattern 1: Executive Report Deck (cho BGĐ)
Slide 1: Title + Date + Author
Slide 2: Executive Summary (3-5 bullets, KẾT LUẬN trước)
Slide 3-7: Key findings (1 slide = 1 finding + supporting data)
Slide 8: Recommendations + Next Steps
Slide 9: Appendix (nếu cần)
**Rule**: ≤10 slides cho monthly, ≤15 cho quarterly

### Pattern 2: Pitch / Proposal Deck (thuyết phục)
Slide 1: Title + Hook question
Slide 2: Problem/Opportunity (pain point, data backing)
Slide 3: Solution (what we propose)
Slide 4-5: How it works / Approach
Slide 6: Benefits / Expected outcomes (quantified)
Slide 7: Timeline + Resources
Slide 8: Ask (budget, approval, next step)
**Rule**: ≤8 slides. Nếu cần thêm → appendix

### Pattern 3: Training / Knowledge Sharing Deck
Slide 1: Title + Learning objectives
Slide 2: Agenda / Outline
Slide 3-N: Content (1 concept per slide, example → principle → application)
Slide N+1: Summary / Key takeaways
Slide N+2: Q&A / Practice exercise
**Rule**: 1 concept per slide, ≤3 bullets per slide

### Pattern 4: Status Update Deck
Slide 1: Title + Reporting period
Slide 2: Dashboard summary (RAG overview)
Slide 3-5: Details per workstream
Slide 6: Risks & Issues
Slide 7: Next period plan
**Rule**: ≤7 slides, heavy on visuals/charts

## Slide Design Rules
- **1 slide = 1 message**: Nếu slide nói 2 thứ → tách
- **Title = Takeaway**: Không viết "Q1 Revenue" → viết "Q1 Revenue tăng 15%, vượt target"
- **6×6 Rule**: Max 6 bullets, mỗi bullet max 6 từ (hướng dẫn, không cứng nhắc)
- **Visual hierarchy**: Title (24-28pt) → Body (18-20pt) → Footnote (12pt)
- **Data viz > table**: Nếu có số liệu → chart hoặc infographic, không bảng thô

## Speaker Notes
Mỗi slide NÊN có speaker notes (2-3 câu) hướng dẫn presenter:
- Điểm chính cần nhấn mạnh
- Câu chuyện / ví dụ minh họa
- Transition sang slide tiếp theo
